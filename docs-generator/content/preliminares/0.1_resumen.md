# ABSTRACT

The administration of access protocols and the monitoring of membership statuses in training or sports entities have historically relied on purely analog schemas. At the "Chely Boops" dance academy in Orizaba, Veracruz, an absolute dependence on manually signed printed cards presented critical weaknesses: severe delays in verification during peak hours, high susceptibility to fraud, and an analytical opacity that obstructed informed strategic decisions.

This applied research documents the conception, design, and deployment of the "Poetry System", a Domain-Driven Design (DDD) software solution that fully digitizes this operational flow. By leveraging fingerprint biometric validation coupled with a Java-based backend architecture (Spring Boot) and a reactive web client, the physical credential validation model was entirely superseded. Statistically, entry latency dropped from intervals of up to 45 seconds per individual to computational verifications taking merely 180 milliseconds. Concurrently, the platform introduces an analytical command center that exposes attendance volume and revenue metrics in real-time.

Empirical results demonstrate total containment against identity spoofing and a direct managerial optimization. Architecturally, adherence to SOLID standards and Clean Architecture guarantees minimal technical debt, enabling the subsequent scaling of the core business logic.

**Keywords:** Biometric Access Control, Domain-Driven Design (DDD), Analytical Dashboards, Spring Boot, Operational Optimization.
